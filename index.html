Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is Fatal Rush Esports?

Fatal Rush Esports is an online competitive tournament hosting platform for gamers where players compete in daily Free Fire & BGMI matches to win coin rewards.

Q2. How do I get Room ID & Password?

Room ID and Password will be displayed inside the match card 15 minutes before the scheduled match start time.

Q3. How do I withdraw my winning coins?

Go to Wallet -> Withdraw Coins -> Select your preferred withdrawal method (UPI or Google Play Redeem Code) -> Enter details and submit.

Q4. When will I get my withdrawal?

Normal withdrawals are processed within 1 to 12 hours. Emergency withdrawals are processed instantly!
