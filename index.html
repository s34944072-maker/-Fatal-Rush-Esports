Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is Fatal Rush Sports?

Fatal Rush Sports is a dedicated tournament platform for participating in various online esports & tournaments to earn rewards.

Q. How to Register in Tournaments?

Simply choose your match from Home screen, click on Join Match, and ensure you have required entry coins.

Q. Are Tournaments Free or Paid?

Some tournaments have entry fees while others are completely free to enter.

Q. How are Winners Determined?

Winners are calculated automatically based on total kills and position placement points in the match.

Q. How to Contact Support?

Click on Contact Us in Profile section or use WhatsApp Floating Icon to reach support team.
